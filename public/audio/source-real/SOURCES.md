# Real Sound Sources

These source files are used to render the `Sound Experience` MP3 variants.

- `applause.ogg` - Wikimedia Commons, "Sound Effects - Applause after a concert.ogg", CC0 1.0, author Amada44.
- `laughter.ogg` - Wikimedia Commons, "Laughter.ogg", public domain, author ezwa / PDSounds.
- `fireworks.ogg` - SoundCamp, "BIG EXPLOSION WITH ELECTRICITY CRACKLES".
- `cash-register.mp3` - SoundCamp, "Cash register ding".
- `bell.mp3` - SoundCamp, church bells sound.
- `windchimes.mp3` - SoundCamp, "wind chimes zaphir 5 seasons sounds".
- Happy Birthday effects use an instrumental generated chime arrangement of the public-domain birthday melody, with no lyrics or vocal recording.

Planned real-source slots:

- `howling-wind.mp3` - real wind ambience for snow/neon/atmosphere.

The generator falls back to synthetic layers only when a real source is unavailable.
